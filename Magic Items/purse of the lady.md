# Purse of the Lady
*Wondrous item, rare*

This luxurious woman’s purse has the incredible ability to create perfume, makeup, tissues, and other items commonly associated with ladies (at the DM’s discretion). A person only needs to mentally picture what she desires while holding the purse for the item to appear inside of it. The amount of women’s products that the bag can create is limited; it can easily cover the daily needs of a fashionable lady, but it cannot create more than 5 gp worth of goods per day.
