# Adventure Introduction

> Relax adventurer, you have landed yourself the cushiest job in the Adventurer's Guild. On the heels of your last great quest, you have proven yourself to be a valuable asset to the Guild and have accepted the coveted position of Town Watch in Evergreen Springs.

> A small mountain town tucked into the wooded nooks of the Verdant Peaks, Evergreen Springs became a popular destination for artists, nobles, and wealthy collectors. Featuring the most galleries per capita of any town on the Sword Coast, simple town guards would simply not suffice for the citizens of Evergreen Springs. Instead, they pay the Guild handsomely to supply proven adventurers on six month rotations, providing security to the residents and businesses. The Guild doesn't hand this assignment out to just anyone, it is a rare opportunity to relax and take in the sights while brushing shoulders with the biggest names in the realm. You spend your days taking in the finest art around, and your nights helping drunken nobles find their room at the Evergreen Hot Springs Retreat. Yep, things are pretty great in this quiet little town. So relax and put your feet up adventurer, you've earned it.
