*Not described in the original adventure*

The LittleHorn Estate sits high on the slope near the back of Evergreen Springs. After traveling through an iron gate, you approach a luxurious estate with wide wings to accommodate visitors. From inside, you are treated to stunning views of the hot springs and valley below.

There should be no reason to explore this location, guests are welcomed into a receiving room obviously intended to take impromptu meetings, with several arrangements of seating available and a writing desk in the corner as well as a standing lectern for a scribe to transcribe meetings.

Lord LittleHorn is an especially influential member of town. If he is already aware of his son's death, well-wishers are already visiting with small gifts and the house is bustling with servants preparing to receive condolences from visitors.
