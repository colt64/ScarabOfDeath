# Ring of Reduction
*Ring, rare* 

This silver band is the unfaithful spouse’s worst nightmare. For as long as you wear this ring, you cannot deliberately tell a lie (no exceptions). 

**Curse**: Once you put this cursed ring around your finger, you can’t remove it unless you are targeted by the wish spell or you cut your finger off
