> > Mortimer Yorman is a necromancer who has been working with Nabulee for years. He has a passion for magical constructs and has learned how to make near-perfect copies of existing people. Mortimer can use a clone spell to duplicate someone’s body, tear it apart, and use the pieces to create a flesh golem that is nearly identical to the original person and that retains a large part of their personality.
