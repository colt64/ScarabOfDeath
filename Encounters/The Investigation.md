# Encounter Description

In this part of the adventure, the characters search for clues that might help them understand how [[Aaron Littlehorn|Aaron]] acquired the scarab of death. The key to solving this mystery is to find discrepancies between what [[Nabulee el Jasafir|Nabulee]] remembers and what the evidence suggests. The PCs should then understand that someone used a spell to alter Nabulee’s memories, which will lead them to [[Mortimer Yorman]]. Clever PCs might also find a way to track down the person who entered Nabulee’s extradimensional vault in the morning. This will also lead them to Mortimer’s house.
