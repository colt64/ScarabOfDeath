# Uncovering the Truth
After the characters interrogate Nabulee’s staff and explore his house, they’ll most certainly want some explanations that the Calishite won’t be able to provide. Eventually, the adventurers will understand that someone who looked just like Nabulee entered the vault, and that someone erased the merchant’s memory.
